I'm a software engineer. I like problems where the hard part is understanding
the system rather than writing the code e.g. distributed protocols, retrieval over
messy data, and lately what it takes to make language models behave predictably
inside real products.

## Now

**Software Engineer at Candle AI** - Austin, Texas, since July 2026.

I work across agentic and full-stack systems: the plumbing that lets language
models take useful actions, and the interfaces people actually touch.

## Previously

**Ribbon Communications** - Bengaluru, June 2025 to July 2026. Software
Engineer II.

I worked on multicast routing for the Neptune enterprise platform, mostly
PIM-SM and MVPN. The work was fault tolerance at scale: keeping connectivity
stable for tens of thousands of concurrent users across geographically
distributed networks, and hunting performance bottlenecks in the routing path.
I also built an internal tool that enforced our coding guidelines using LLMs 
as the reviewer, which took a meaningful bite out of review time.

**Candle AI** - January to May 2025, as an intern, and briefly in mid-2024 when
the company was still Nava AI.

I built an LLM evaluation framework with custom benchmarking datasets, running
validation workflows on AWS Step Functions. I also led a SOC 2 Type II
compliance effort across the cloud infrastructure, moving it to a zero-trust
architecture that cleared external audit with no remediation items. Earlier, on
the Nava AI product, I worked on an immigration chatbot: retrieval-augmented
generation, Supabase-backed auth, and the unglamorous work of making generated
answers actually good.

**The Internet Archive** - Google Summer of Code 2024, May to August.

I built Athena, an AI-powered tool for exploring web archives, against a corpus
of over 50 million pages. The interesting problem was query routing: getting
useful answers out of 100GB+ of historical data through the Wayback Machine's
infrastructure without waiting all afternoon for them. It ended up roughly an
order of magnitude faster than the search methods it replaced, wrapped in a
Streamlit interface that turned multi-hour manual archive queries into something
a researcher could run in seconds.

**IndiaAI Fellow** - December 2024 to May 2025. A fellowship from the Ministry
of Electronics and Information Technology, awarded to a small cohort drawn from
the top 100 NIRF-ranked engineering colleges doing AI research.

**Centre of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence**, NIT Calicut - research
intern, June to September 2024, on the SAMUDRA project with the Smart Security
Systems team.

**CITRA** (Centre for Information Technology, Research, and Automation) -

social networks, and presented it to the Bureau of Police Research and
Development under the Ministry of Home Affairs.
