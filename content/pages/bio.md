title: "Bio"
---

I'm a software engineer at Candle AI in Austin, where I work on AI and
full-stack systems. I'm interested in the space between models and the systems
around them: how they are built, how they behave in practice, and how we can
make them useful.

I studied Electronics and Communications Engineering at NIT Calicut.
I became interested in machine learning along the way, and eventually found 
myself drawn to natural language processing, particularly to the question of 
how language models learn to represent meaning and capture the relationships 
between words in language across and how it maps out in different languages.

My path since then has moved between a few different kinds of engineering. At
CITRA, I worked on machine-learning applications for detecting impersonation on
social networks. Through Google Summer of Code at the Internet Archive, I worked
with the Wayback Machine on AI-driven analysis and exploration of web archives.
I later spent a year at Ribbon Communications working on networking software
and protocols.

These days, I'm mostly interested in AI systems, distributed systems, and
networking. I write here mostly to work through ideas that I find interesting,
and occasionally to document things I've learned along the way.
